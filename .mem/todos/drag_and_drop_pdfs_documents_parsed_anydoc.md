---
title: Drag-and-drop PDFs and documents, parsed with anydoc
created_at: "2026-10-28T16:48:01+02:00"
---

Dragging a PDF file into the app should work and show a PDF icon. The actual parsing should use anydoc (https://github.com/firecrawl/anydoc); the same goes for PowerPoint, Word, EPUB and similar documents.
